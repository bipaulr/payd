# P4. Risk engine

Read CLAUDE.md and docs/PLAN.md. Build the risk engine milestone.

In lib/risk, write one pure function per check. Each takes the bill and the
vendor's history and returns null or a RiskFlag with a severity and a
plain-language reason that names the evidence.

1. duplicate: same vendor and invoice number, or same vendor, total and
   issue date. The file hash differing must not hide a duplicate.
2. payee_changed: PayPal email on the bill differs from the email used in
   this vendor's past payouts. Always high severity.
3. amount_anomaly: total is more than 3x the vendor's median, when the
   vendor has at least three past bills.
4. math_mismatch: line items, tax and total disagree by more than one cent.
5. lookalike_vendor: a new vendor whose name or email is within edit
   distance 2 of a known vendor.
6. pressure_language: one model call that looks for urgency and changed
   payment details in the payment instructions text.

Then:
- assessBill(bill) runs all checks and sets low, medium or high. Rules
  decide the level. The model call never lowers it.
- One model call writes a two-sentence summary of the flags for the owner.
  If it fails, fall back to joining the reasons.
- High risk bills cannot be approved without a typed override reason, saved
  on the Approval.
- Unit tests for every check, including the clean case and edge cases such
  as a vendor with no history. Mock the model calls in tests.

Example reason: "Past 6 payments to Asha Traders went to
accounts@ashatraders.example. This bill asks for
asha.traders.pay@mail.example."

Final check: typecheck, lint and tests exit 0, with at least one passing
and one failing case tested for each of the six checks.
