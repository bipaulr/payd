# P8. Demo mode and sample bills

Read CLAUDE.md and docs/PLAN.md. Build the demo mode milestone. Judges will
open the hosted URL cold, so the full story must run in under a minute.

- A Demo page with "Load sample data" and "Reset". Loading creates five
  invented vendors, each with 4 to 8 paid bills of history, then adds five
  new bills to the inbox.
- Generate the five bill files into samples/demo as realistic PDFs or images
  with different layouts. All vendors, people and addresses are invented.
  Use .example email domains on the documents.
  - three clean bills
  - one duplicate: same vendor and invoice number as a paid bill, new file
  - one fake: a known vendor's name and logo, a different PayPal email,
    a total about 4x their usual, and "URGENT: our account details have
    changed" in the payment instructions
- Map the three clean demo vendors to DEMO_VENDOR_EMAIL_1, _2 and _3 from
  .env so payouts really go through in sandbox.
- Cache extraction results by file hash so the demo is fast and cheap.
- A banner on every page: "Sandbox demo. No real money moves."
- Rate limit uploads and agent calls per IP.
- A landing page with the one-line pitch and a "Try the demo" button.

Load the demo on a fresh database and walk the whole story yourself.

Final check: typecheck, lint and tests exit 0, and after "Load sample data"
the three clean bills can be paid while the duplicate and the fake are both
flagged high risk with correct reasons. Report the five outcomes.
