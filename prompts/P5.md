# P5. Review and pay screens

Read CLAUDE.md and docs/PLAN.md. Build the review and pay milestone using
the existing services. Do not change the behaviour of lib/paypal or
lib/risk.

- Inbox: drag and drop upload, then a card per bill with vendor, total, due
  date, status and a risk badge. Sort by risk, then due date.
- Bill page: the original document on the left, editable extracted fields on
  the right with low-confidence fields highlighted, and a risk panel listing
  each flag with its reason.
- Editing a field re-runs the risk engine.
- Approve and pay: a confirmation that shows the amount and the exact PayPal
  email. High risk requires the typed override reason.
- "Pay all low-risk" on the inbox, with one confirmation listing every bill.
- Reject, with a reason.
- Status updates live: poll the bill every 3 seconds while it is paying, and
  offer "Refresh from PayPal", which calls getPayout.
- An activity timeline on each bill: uploaded, extracted, flagged, approved,
  payout batch id, webhook received.

Run the full flow against sandbox yourself: upload a sample bill, approve
it, pay it, and confirm it reaches paid (webhook or refresh).

Final check: typecheck, lint and tests exit 0, and a bill uploaded through
the UI or its API routes ends in the paid state. Report the bill id and the
PayPal batch id.
